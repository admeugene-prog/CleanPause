# How to clean your keyboard and mouse
1. **Use alcohol wipes**, suitable for cleaning electronics.
2. **Wipe the keyboard** — clean each key and the surface between the keys.
3. **Clean the mouse** — wipe its body, buttons, scroll wheel and underside.
4. **Let everything dry completely** before using your devices.
> **Important:** CleanPause temporarily blocks keyboard and mouse input to prevent accidental keystrokes and clicks while cleaning. Input will return automatically after 5 minutes.
**Just 5 minutes — and your devices are clean again!**
## Why clean your devices regularly?
- **Bacteria and microbes.** Keyboards and mice constantly come into contact with your hands. Microorganisms can accumulate on their surfaces and transfer to your hands.
- **Grease and dirt.** Fingerprints and skin oils gradually build up on keys and the mouse body.
- **Device performance.** Dirt between keys or around the sensor can interfere with normal operation.
Regular cleaning helps maintain workspace hygiene and comfortable device use. A dirty personal keyboard does not automatically mean a high risk of illness.
## How often should you clean your devices?
| Usage | Suggested frequency |
|---|---|
| Personal home computer | Every 1–2 weeks |
| Daily office use | Every week |
| Shared keyboard and mouse | More often, ideally between users |
| Visible dirt | Immediately |
*These intervals are reminders, not mandatory hygiene standards. Follow the device manufacturer's guidance on suitable cleaning products.*
